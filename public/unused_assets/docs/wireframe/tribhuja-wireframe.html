Specifications

Built on what holds.

RCC shear-wall framed structure. Autoclaved aerated concrete.
Designed for wind and seismic loads.

Structure & Shell

Main Doors
Hardwood frame with teak veneer, melamine / PU polish.
Windows
UPVC profile, tinted toughened / HS glass. Mosquito mesh provision.
Flooring
800×800mm double-sized vitrified tiles. Marble / granite at entrance lounge.
External
Textured finish, two coats premium exterior emulsion.

Fittings & Finish

Kitchen
Granite platform, stainless steel sink. Separate municipal & bore water supply.
Bathrooms
Vanity basin on granite counter. Wall-mounted EWC. False ceiling throughout.
CP Fittings
Single-lever fixtures. Wall mixer with shower. Reputed-make sanitaryware.
Electrical
3-phase supply, individual meter boards. FRLS copper wiring. Modular switches.

Building Systems

Lifts
Four high-speed passenger lifts with V3F energy efficiency & rescue device.
Power Backup
100% DG set backup with acoustic enclosure & AMF.
Water & STP
Water-softening plant. STP. Rainwater harvesting as per norms.
Fire & Safety
Hydrant & sprinkler systems. Smoke detectors. NBC-compliant throughout.
Security
IoT-enabled BMS. 24/7 surveillance. Panic buttons in all lifts.
