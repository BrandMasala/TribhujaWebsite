The Arc

The flow follows a deliberate six-phase arc borrowed from the catalogue: attract → orient → scale → qualify → trust → convert. Each phase corresponds to a different user mindset. Sections can't be reordered without breaking the psychology.

Content Types

Three tones alternate — emotion → fact → signature. Emotion opens sections (establishes register). Fact grounds them (earns trust). Signature claims anchor them (commercial weight). The rhythm prevents the site from reading as either too poetic or too corporate.

Why no Tatva-Tantra

Per your instruction, the triadic meta-section is removed. Its work — lineage, method, form — is now distributed across the remaining sections. The emblem in the hero carries the triad visually; no copy section is needed.
